If AI is going to get good at everything, what's the point of putting effort into learning anything?

To cope with this, I'm telling myself that the skill will probably become useless, but I'm still going to put the same love and effort into getting better at it, because I have the capacity to learn and love as many things as I choose to. Right now, I'm learning Rust and networking concepts to get the Cloudflare role with the ZTC team.
